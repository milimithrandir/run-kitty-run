Run Kitty Run

Run Kitty Run is a free online co-op game you play in your browser. You are a kitty in a labyrinth full of wolves. Reach the heart of the labyrinth, don't touch the wolves, and never leave a kitty behind.

What kind of game is it?

It is a team dodging game. The map is one long corridor that winds inwards as a square spiral, and every lane is full of wolves. A single touch from a wolf and your kitty is down. A downed kitty leaves a circle behind, and any teammate who runs through that circle brings it back. One kitty reaching the goal is enough to clear the level for everyone, so the whole game is about covering for each other: somebody runs ahead, somebody doubles back for the fallen.

What's in the game

  • Three modes. Run + Skate plays every level twice, on foot by day and on skates by night. Run only and Skate only are each half on its own.
  • Normal or Easy. In an easy game the level starts over when everyone is down, instead of the run being over.
  • Online lobbies for up to 32 kitties. Share a 4-letter code or an invite link. Friends can join a run that is already going.
  • Phones and computers together. Everyone plays on the same server, whatever they're holding.
  • Items. Speed boots, a shield and an extra life, scattered along the lanes.
  • Things to earn. Sunglasses, rainbow boots and rainbow skates for clearing level 8, a new song and more for beating the game, a medic badge for the kitty who keeps reviving everyone, and a Legends board for the teams that beat the game.
  • Controller support, your own key bindings, and graphics settings down to Ultra Low for slow laptops.

The full rules, controls and a few tips are on the how to play page.

Questions

Is Run Kitty Run free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser at runkittyrun.fun. There is nothing to download or install, no ads and no account needed.

Do I need Warcraft 3 to play?

No. Run Kitty Run is its own game that runs in your web browser. You don't need Warcraft 3, and you don't need any account at all to play: the swag account is completely optional. Just hop in and play.

How many players can play together?

Up to 32 kitties in one online lobby. You can also play solo, or two players in local co-op on one keyboard.

How do I play with friends?

Open runkittyrun.fun, choose Multiplayer and create a lobby. Share the 4-letter lobby code or the invite link, and start the run when everyone has joined. Friends can also join a run that is already going.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The game runs in the browser on phones and tablets: turn the phone sideways and steer with your thumb. Phone and computer players can join the same lobby.
