How to play Run Kitty Run

Reach the heart of the labyrinth. Don't touch the wolves. Never leave a kitty behind. That's the whole game; the rest of this page is detail.

▶ PLAY NOW Free in your browser · 1 to 32 players

The rules

  1. Follow the spiral. Every level is one long corridor that winds inwards to a goal room in the middle. You start in the outer corner.
  2. One touch and you're down. Wolves roam every lane. They aren't hunting you; they wander, stop and set off again, and you slip through the gaps.
  3. Revive your friends. A downed kitty leaves a circle where it fell. Any teammate who runs over the circle brings it back, and it is safe for a moment afterwards.
  4. Take the crown. In the goal room, the glowing victory circle is safe ground. Grab the crown in its middle to clear the level for the whole team.
  5. If everyone is down, the run is over. In an Easy game the level starts over instead.

Where you're safe

  • Safe squares. The paved squares at the corners of the spiral are wolf-free. Wolves walk right up to the edge, but never onto the tiles.
  • Checkpoints. Each level has two flags. When one kitty reaches a flag, everyone who is down comes back and the team gathers there.
  • Trees. On autumn levels you can climb the trees. A kitty under the branches can't be reached, and every tree holds a pair of boots.
  • The victory circle in the goal room. You can run back out of it to help a friend; you're only safe while you're inside.
  • The first two seconds of a level, and a moment after every revive.

The three modes

Run + Skate

The full game. Every level is played twice: first on foot by day, then on ice skates by night. Reaching the goal by day is a checkpoint that brings everyone back and moves the team onto the night rink.

Run only

Just the levels on foot. Wolves here are wanderers: they roam, rest and head off again, and on later levels they move faster and rest less.

Skate only

Just the ice, through winter, spring, summer and autumn under floodlights. On ice a kitty keeps sliding the way it faces and can only carve a turn, so you can't stop and you can't flip around. The wolves here move in patterns: rows that sweep from wall to wall and chargers that race down a lane. You learn the pattern, wait for the gap and commit.

Normal or Easy

You pick one under the mode. In a Normal game the run ends when every kitty is down at once. In an Easy game the level starts over instead, so the team can keep trying. Easy games have their own stats and their own rewards.

Playing online with friends

  1. Open runkittyrun.fun and choose Multiplayer.
  2. Create a lobby and pick the mode. A lobby can be public or private (with a password).
  3. Send your friends the 4-letter lobby code or the invite link. Up to 32 kitties fit in one lobby.
  4. The host starts the run. Late friends can join a run that is already going.

No friends online? The Multiplayer screen lists the open public lobbies, and you can jump into any of them. Nobody needs an account, and phones, tablets and computers all play together.

Local is for one screen: play solo, or two players in co-op with one on the mouse and one on the keys.

Tips

  • Revive your friends. A kitty that's down comes back when a teammate runs over its circle, so go back for each other.
  • Take your time. There's no timer. Sometimes waiting on a safe square for the right moment is the best approach.
  • Watch how the wolves move. See where they go before you step out.
  • Music makes you go faster.
  • Play with friends. The game is made to be beaten together.
  • On ice, turn early. You carry your speed, so start the curve before the gap opens. If it closes, skate in a circle and come round again.

What you can earn

Clearing level 8 unlocks new looks for your kitty in the mode you earned them in: sunglasses, rainbow boots and rainbow skates. Beating the game unlocks a new song for the soundtrack, and more! Revive enough teammates and you get a medic badge on your player card. Teams that beat the game are listed on the Legends board on the main menu.

The game is free, and you never need an account to play. A swag account is optional: you activate it on the website by paying any amount you like once, from €0.50, and it keeps your stats and unlocks.
